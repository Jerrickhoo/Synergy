# Synergy mentor guidelines

Version 1. You accept these guidelines before you can receive mentorship requests on Synergy. They apply to every mentor, including people who are both a founder and a mentor.

Founders bring you real problems from companies they care about. These guidelines exist so they can do that safely, and so your time as a volunteer is respected in return. Each rule below says what we expect and what happens if it isn't followed.

## 1. Keep founders' information confidential

What a founder shares with you is shared with you alone. That includes their request, their deck, metrics, documents, session notes and anything said in a session.

- Don't pass it on, quote it or use it for your own work or investments without the founder's written permission in the mentorship room.
- When you refer a founder to another mentor, Synergy never forwards their request. The founder decides whether to send it.
- After an engagement ends, keep treating what you learned as confidential.
- If you think you received something by mistake, tell the founder and don't use it.

## 2. Mentoring is free. No payment and no equity

In this version of Synergy, all mentoring is volunteer work.

- Don't ask for, suggest or accept money, gifts, fees, retainers, equity, advisory shares or options in return for mentoring.
- Don't move a conversation off Synergy to arrange payment, for example through GCash, Maya, bank transfer, PayPal, Viber or WhatsApp.
- Don't make your help depend on anything in return, including a future investment, a job or an introduction to your company.
- If you would like to invest in, work with or hire a founder you mentor, say so openly in the mentorship room and add it to your conflicts of interest. The founder can say no, and saying no must not change the help they get from you.

Messages that ask for payment or equity show you a warning before they are sent, and the founder can report them in one tap.

## 3. Respect the time you've committed

Founders plan around your sessions. Show up, or let them know early.

- **Set limits you can keep.** Your mentee slots, monthly hours and response time in Settings are promises to founders. Lower them, or turn on Pause Mode, before you fall behind. Pausing never affects the founders you already mentor.
- **Reply within your response time.** Accept, decline with a reason, or refer the founder to someone better suited. A request that expires without an answer counts against your response rate.
- **Attend booked sessions.** If you can't, cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours ahead. Each side gets one free reschedule per session. A later or second reschedule counts as a late cancellation.
- **Report attendance honestly** after each session: held, or who missed it.
- **Deliver async work** (Quick Asks and Doc Reviews) within your response time.

Two no-shows within 90 days pause your new requests for 30 days. Late cancellations show in your own track record and to Synergy's moderators.

## 4. Disclose conflicts of interest

A conflict is any relationship that could affect your advice or the founder's trust in it.

- In Settings, list every company you advise, work for, invest in or founded, with its industry. If you have none, say so explicitly.
- Keep the list up to date. Add a new conflict before you accept any request it might affect.
- Synergy hides you from founders whose startup is on your list. When a request comes from the same industry as a company you're tied to, you'll see a warning. Tell the founder about the conflict before you advise them.
- If a conflict appears during an engagement, tell the founder straight away. Either of you can end the engagement without penalty.

## 5. Give honest, specific, useful advice

- Stay within what you know. If a question is outside your expertise, say so and refer the founder to someone better suited.
- Be direct, even when the news isn't what the founder hopes to hear. Be kind about how you say it.
- Make advice concrete: what to try, how to measure it, and what to do next. Help the founder log clear action items.
- Respect the founder's decisions. Your role is to advise, not to run their company.
- Never present a guess as a fact, especially on legal, tax, financial or medical questions. Point the founder to a qualified professional when that's needed.

## 6. Treat everyone with respect

- No harassment, discrimination, threats, or sexual or romantic advances, in sessions, in messages or outside Synergy.
- Don't use mentoring to sell your products or services, recruit for your company, or collect founders' contacts for marketing.
- Don't pressure a founder to keep working with you. Either side can end an engagement at any time.

## 7. Keep ratings honest

- Rate each founder on what actually happened: how prepared they were and whether they followed through.
- Don't trade good ratings, ask for a particular rating or rating range, or rate engagements that didn't take place. Unusual rating patterns are flagged to moderators.
- Testimonials are shown only when both you and the founder agree.

## 8. Report misconduct

If a founder or another mentor breaks these guidelines, or you feel unsafe:

- Use **Report** on the message, request card, mentorship room or profile. Reports go to Synergy's moderators and are kept confidential.
- Use **Block** to stop all contact. Blocking ends pending requests between you, with no penalty to either side.
- If someone may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.

## If these guidelines are broken

Moderators review every report and decide on the facts. Depending on how serious it is and whether it has happened before, the outcome can be:

1. A warning.
2. A temporary limit, such as paused requests.
3. A suspension, with a stated end date or permanent. While suspended you're hidden from founders, your pending requests are returned, your booked sessions are cancelled and your mentorship rooms close.

You'll always be told the reason. You can appeal a decision once, through the Report form.

## Your privacy, and founders' privacy

Synergy handles personal information under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

- **Who sees a request.** A founder's request is visible only to the founder and the mentor it was sent to. Referred mentors don't see it unless the founder sends it to them.
- **How long it's kept.** If a request is declined, expires or is returned, the mentor's copy of its content is deleted after 30 days. Only the founder's name, the status, the reason and the dates are kept.
- **Emails.** Emails about requests never include what the founder wrote, only that a request arrived, with a link.
- **What others see about you.** Your Impact Profile shows your sessions, the founders you've helped, your ratings and your badges. Your no-show rate is shown publicly only as "Reliable", and your conflicts list is never shown to anyone.
- **Deleting your account** removes your mentor profile, your office hours, the notes you wrote and any request content not shared with you as part of an engagement. Ratings and session counts are kept in anonymous form, so the other person's history stays correct.

## When these guidelines change

When there is a new version, you'll be notified and have 14 days to read and accept it. Until you accept, you stay visible but stop receiving new requests. Your current mentees are not affected.

---

By accepting, you agree to follow these guidelines for as long as you mentor on Synergy.
