## Implementation choices made for owner review

- Ongoing cycles last 8 weeks; their due date is a check-in target, not forced completion. Existing accepted ongoing rooms start cycle 1 at upgrade when no cycle exists. Both participants must review and decide. Two Continue choices advance; either Finish ends after both respond. Each new cycle starts with empty goals and sessions; previous goals, proof, schedules and feedback remain in cycle history, while optional task cards remain available. Cycle reviews do not inflate public ratings. The final review still has its own 14-day window. A missing cycle response currently waits for the partner; normal nudge and closure tools remain available. Milestone work freezes once private cycle feedback begins, preserving the work being reviewed. Fictional preview partners submit labelled simulated cycle reviews and mirror the founder's Continue/Finish choice. Moderator confirmation of a final disputed milestone opens cycle review on both room copies.
- Undo clears both confirmations on the final milestone, leaving earlier work, proof and schedules intact. For ongoing mentorship it preserves cycle feedback and clears the cycle decisions so they can be chosen again after reconfirmation. It does not immediately relaunch. It is unavailable at exactly 24 hours or after either final review.
- Moderator deadlines use UTC weekdays and exclude weekends, without national holidays. Legacy pending affiliation records without timestamps get a one-time startup timestamp. Existing launched rooms use updatedAt when launchedAt is absent.
- New accounts get weekly digests. Explicit preferences are preserved; legacy absent preferences remain Off. Match notification IDs identify a person rather than a fluctuating score. The in-app digest shows the current day/week of first-seen matches, sorted by current score.
- Email outbox is a durable integration boundary with a non-delivering stub; an actual worker/provider remains future work. Password recovery is deferred by explicit owner choice.
- Existing confirmed choices, including Profile visibility in Danger zone, remain unchanged. No weights were retuned. Proposed product wording requires owner approval.
