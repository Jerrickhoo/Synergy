# Founder rundown script

A start-to-finish pass through Synergy as a startup founder: sign up, set up the startup, find help, complete a mentorship and a founder exchange, then come back later. It takes about 40 minutes.

## Before you start

- Run `npm run dev` and open http://localhost:5173. Local preview mode is the only setup where one person can finish the whole flow (see [Hosted site](#hosted-site)).
- Use an email you have not used before.
- Keep the [Findings log](#findings-log) open and record every spot where you hesitate, get confused or hit an error. The findings are the main output of the rundown.

**Your character:** you run _Shiftly_, a seed-stage startup with a pricing and churn problem.

## Act 1: Sign up and set up the startup (about 5 min)

1. On the sign-in screen, choose **Founder**, enter your new email and a password, and select **Sign in**.
2. On _How do you want to start?_, choose **Set up my startup**. Don't pick the sample; a real founder wouldn't.
3. Paste this into the description box:

   > Shiftly is staff scheduling software for independent restaurants. We are a seed-stage team of 6 with 40 paying restaurants, but churn spikes after month two. We need help with pricing strategy and customer retention. We can offer intros to restaurant operators and product feedback.

4. Select **Suggest profile details**. Check that it suggests Startup name "Shiftly", Stage Seed, and needs of Pricing strategy and Customer retention.
5. Select **Apply suggestions**. Fill **Your name** and anything else still unticked in the Required details ring, then select **Save profile**.
6. Refresh the page and confirm the profile is still there.

## Act 2: Find help (about 5 min)

7. Go to **Home** and read what it tells you to do next. Note whether that is clear.
8. Open **Matches** and compare **Suggested** with **Browse all**. You should see pricing and retention mentors such as Marcus Webb and Hana Sato.
9. Open a mentor, then open **Why this match** and **See the factor breakdown**. Ask yourself whether you would trust the score.
10. Try a search, a filter and **Clear filters**. **Save for later** one profile.
11. If Matches offers **Get notified when someone offers…**, turn it on.

## Act 3: Get mentorship (about 10 min)

12. Open **Marcus Webb** (pricing) and choose **Single Session**, or the closest format he offers. Fill in the request:
    - Problem: "Restaurants churn after month two on our per-location plan."
    - What we tried: "We interviewed eight churned customers and added an annual discount."
    - Question: "Should we move to per-seat pricing or add a lower tier?"
13. Select **Send mentorship request**. Read the pending card, then select **Simulate acceptance** in the dashed _Prototype only_ box.
14. In the Mentorship room, add a note in **Session prep** and select **Share preparation**.
15. Select **Simulate mentor confirming the goal**. Open **Schedule**, pick tomorrow at 10:00, save it, then select **Get calendar file** and **Download .ics**.
16. Add one action item, for example "Draft per-seat pricing page".
17. Select **Simulate the session taking place**. Fill in **Founder follow-up**, marking the action item Done or In progress, and select **Submit follow-up**.
18. Select **Write review**, then **Submit private review**. Check that both reviews are released and the room shows **Reviewed**.

## Act 4: Trade with another startup (about 12 min)

19. In **Matches → Browse all**, search for **Brightlane** (or Fairtab). Choose **Beta Testing Exchange** and select **Send introduction**.
20. In **Introductions**, check that it is pending, then select **Simulate acceptance**. Open the Deal Room.
21. In **Terms**, fill Shared goal, Scope and deliverables, Timeline, Commitment and Exchange. Select **Save draft**, then **Accept these terms**, then use the _Prototype only_ box to have the partner accept.
22. In the **Task board**, add two tasks with an owner, priority and due date. Move one to **Done**.
23. Under **Milestones**, work through all five: Scope defined, Testers assigned, Testing round, Feedback submitted and Issues reviewed. Use **Attach proof** where it is required (upload a small .txt or PDF at least once), select **Mark as done**, and have the partner confirm each one.
24. When the last milestone is confirmed by both of you, the room should switch to **Launched** on its own. Submit your private review and check that the room reaches **Reviewed**.
25. Send one chat message in the room.

## Act 5: Daily use and coming back (about 5 min)

26. Open **Updates**, switch between **All** and **Unread**, open one update, then select **Mark all as read**.
27. In **Settings**, switch to Dark appearance and back, and check that your match alert is listed.
28. Sign out. Then sign back in with the same email and password and check that your profile, both rooms and both reviews are still there.
29. Resize the window to phone width (about 390px) and repeat one task, such as opening a room from Home.

## Goal: when the rundown counts as done

The rundown is done when all eight of these are true. Tick each one as you confirm it.

| #   | Done when…                                                                                                                        | Done |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---- |
| 1   | Your profile was built from your own description (not the sample) and the Required details ring is full                           | [ ]  |
| 2   | Suggested showed at least one mentor who covers a need you stated, and you opened **Why this match**                              | [ ]  |
| 3   | One **Mentorship room** shows **Reviewed**, and both reviews are visible                                                          | [ ]  |
| 4   | One **Deal Room** shows **Reviewed**, with all 5 milestones confirmed by both sides and at least one proof file attached          | [ ]  |
| 5   | You downloaded a calendar file for a scheduled session                                                                            | [ ]  |
| 6   | **Updates** shows 0 unread                                                                                                        | [ ]  |
| 7   | After signing out and back in, items 1–6 are still true                                                                           | [ ]  |
| 8   | The findings log lists every friction point with its step number, and the run has a 1–5 "would a real founder finish this?" score | [ ]  |

If you can't finish a step, record it as **Blocked at step N** with a screenshot and keep going where you can. A rundown with honest blocked steps still counts. Skipping a step quietly doesn't.

## Hosted site

The hosted site (synergy-prototype.netlify.app) runs with real accounts, so the sample startups, mentors and _Simulate_ buttons are not there. You need to verify your email, Matches only lists other real users, and sending introductions or mentorship requests needs a moderator to verify your affiliation (Profile → Verification). If `SYNERGY_ADMIN_EMAILS` is empty, nobody can request that, so both stay locked. On your own, you can only get through Act 2 there. To run the full script on the live site, you also need a second (mentor) account and a moderator listed in `SYNERGY_ADMIN_EMAILS`.
